Chapter 1: Bedtime Stories - The Legend Of The Beast

Written by: Wolcram Mayers And "The Rages Band" — Feb 11, 2020

Once upon a time, there came a needy friend named Beastiee at "Home Town Rolling School". He was teased relentlessly by classmates who called his face ugly and beast-like. Frustrated and lonely, Beastiee struggled to find true, humble friends.

Suddenly, the wise Wizard Polymer appeared. He reminded Beastiee to maintain a good heart, humility, and confidence, assuring him that bad karma would soon catch up to the arrogant bullies.

When the classmates continued their cruelty, Polymer transformed the disrespectful bullies into actual wild Beasts, while granting Beastiee a handsome face and true intelligent friends.
